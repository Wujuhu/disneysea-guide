# Map surface

Mode: Operate. Local HTML guide for first-time Chinese visitors, official map first, practical guidance second.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A harbour visitor centre, with a readable attraction index beside a geographically faithful illustrated map.

OWN-WORLD: White and mist blue surfaces, deep marine ink, teal active controls, ochre for optional paid access. Consistent outlined icons and strong Chinese type.

STORY: Recognise regions, locate an attraction, understand it in one sentence, then follow a dated five-attraction and boat-show plan.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-height shell with a collapsible side rail and independently collapsible attraction index. Map orientation toggles between north-up and official artwork direction; pins and routes follow, Chinese labels remain upright. Original English names appear only in the selected detail. The index separates attractions, shows, restaurants and transport; dining uses two groups: reservable seating and no seating reservation, with mobile-order venues and stalls included in the latter. Mobile ordering is an additional label, not a third group. Peter Pan is excluded by default. Restaurants use utensil markers (17px), snack stalls use awning markers (15px), and other non-route dots are 12px; primary route and show markers keep their sizes. Seating-booking details give only advance release dates, simple steps and the official booking link. Mobile-order details name Tokyo Disney Resort App and its ordering steps; venue handling, same-day vacancies and meal-planning advice are omitted. Show details and guide lists separately display dated past reference times without using them as selected-date schedules. Green rings mark date-eligible DPA facilities. The Hidden category always lists date-confirmed closures and user-hidden records with reasons; one Show hidden switch restores both kinds throughout regular lists, map and guides. Unpublished show times remain visible. Route names and the short show labels stay visible; other names appear on hover/focus. The entry is node 0 and connects to the first play activity; subsequent numbers and connecting lines include only play activities; shows keep their large standalone boat/moon markers and restaurants keep utensil markers. The timetable has a 940px maximum measure and 24px desktop lane gap. It follows a counterclockwise loop including Fantasy Springs, with no show or restaurant route points. Meal breaks stay flexible. DPA assumptions and booking times are explicit; notes connect in a parallel lane; mobile indents notes below the related activity. Orientation and panel preferences persist locally.
